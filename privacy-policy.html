Privacy Policy

Daily Game Performance Report · Effective September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Game Performance Report automation (the “Service”) handles information when it creates and delivers a daily game-performance report.

Purpose

The Service prepares a daily HTML report from authorized, read-only game-performance data and emails the validated report to its configured recipient. It is a private operational reporting tool, not a consumer-facing service.

Information the Service uses

  • Authorized game-performance records required to calculate the report.
  • A Google OAuth authorization for the designated sender mailbox, used only to send the report and confirm that the sent message is present in that mailbox’s Sent label.
  • The sender, recipient, report date, delivery status, message identifier, and file hash recorded in the local automation receipt for delivery verification and troubleshooting.

How Gmail data is used

Gmail access is used solely to send the generated report attachment from the designated sender mailbox and to verify delivery through the Sent label. The Service does not sell Gmail data, use Gmail data for advertising, or transfer Gmail data to third parties except for sending the report to the configured recipient.

Storage and security

OAuth authorization material and automation receipts are stored locally on the authorized reporting computer. The report automation retains only the information needed to create, validate, deliver, and troubleshoot the report. Access is limited to the authorized reporting workflow.

Sharing

The daily report is sent only to the configured recipient. Public report pages, when published, contain the report content selected for publication; they do not expose Gmail authorization material.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the reporting workflow changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact dailygameperformancereport@gmail.com.
